GemFusion Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

GemFusion is a gem-merging game developed by Akmal Ali. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Information we collect

GemFusion does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data removes it.

Permissions

GemFusion does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Third-party services

Google Play may collect diagnostic and usage information as part of the platform. That collection is governed by Google's privacy policy. The GemFusion app itself does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children's privacy

GemFusion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because GemFusion does not collect personal data, there is no server-side data to delete. You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact akmal.ali.software@gmail.com.
